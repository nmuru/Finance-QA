Financial Resilience Analysis — Infosys Ltd (SEC CIK 1067491)

Scope and basis: Annual statement-of-financial-position (balance-sheet "instant") data and annual income/cash-flow data for fiscal years FY2021–FY2025, as reported in SEC Form 20-F XBRL filings (IFRS taxonomy, fiscal year-end March 31). All figures in USD.

---

1. Risk Summary

Infosys carries a structurally defensive balance sheet: current assets cover current liabilities by roughly 2.3x, the company reports no material interest-bearing borrowings, and operating profit covers total finance costs by more than 80x. Bankruptcy risk from liquidity or debt-service stress is assessed as very low. The main forward-looking risk factors are not solvency but cash-deployment pressure — rising dividend payouts (≈$2.42B in FY2025) and a lower cash buffer in FY2023 that has since been rebuilt — and the classification of lease liabilities (~$0.96B) as the most debt-like obligation on the statement. No ratio crosses any distress threshold in the trailing three years.

Data Limitations & Caveats

  • Infosys files SEC Form 20-F under the IFRS taxonomy; the US-GAAP concepts contemplated by a standard US analysis (e.g., AssetsCurrent, LiabilitiesCurrent, DebtCurrent, StockholdersEquity) are not the populated tags. IFRS equivalents were used: CurrentAssets, CurrentLiabilities, EquityAttributableToOwnersOfParent, Borrowings, and FinanceCosts.
  • The Borrowings concept is populated as nil, and no long-term borrowings concept carries observations for FY2021–FY2025; total debt is therefore assessed at ~$0 per the stated analytical convention. Total liabilities should not be misread as debt — they include trade payables, tax liabilities, provisions, and lease liabilities.
  • No inventory concept is reported; quick ratio equals current ratio under the assumption of nil inventory.
  • Certain granular line items (e.g., full decomposed current-asset schedule) are not fully populated in the XBRL facts for all years; the analysis uses the populated concepts above plus the normalized statement rows (total assets, total liabilities, goodwill, income-statement and cash-flow lines) supplied in the controlled evidence set.
