## Skill: Cross-Statement Analysis
**Objective:** Test whether major movements across the three statements are directionally consistent.

**Instructions:**
1. Retrieve income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement evidence.
2. Compare major changes in revenue, operating income, net income, assets, liabilities, equity, and cash.
3. Identify material movements that merit investigation because they are unusually large, reverse prior trends, or appear disconnected from related statements.
4. Separate arithmetic relationships supported by the statements from explanations that require additional evidence.
5. Present each signal with the exact periods and reported measures that create it.
