## Skill: Earnings Cash Conversion
**Objective:** Assess the relationship between accounting earnings and cash generation.

**Instructions:**
1. Retrieve the income statement and cash flow statement.
2. Compare net income with operating cash flow for each available annual period.
3. Calculate a simple operating-cash-flow-to-net-income ratio when both measures are available and meaningful.
4. Identify persistent or abrupt divergence across years.
5. Treat divergence as a diagnostic signal. Do not state that it proves earnings manipulation or an accounting problem.
