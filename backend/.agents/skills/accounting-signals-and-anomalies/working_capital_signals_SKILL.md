## Skill: Working Capital Signals
**Objective:** Identify unusual historical changes in operating working capital.

**Instructions:**
1. Retrieve the annual income statement and balance sheet.
2. Compare revenue with receivables and inventory trends when those line items are available.
3. Calculate year-over-year changes for major working-capital accounts where the data supports it.
4. Flag large divergences for review, especially rapid balance-sheet growth relative to the related income-statement measure.
5. Do not infer collection problems, channel stuffing, inventory obsolescence, or other causes without supporting evidence.
