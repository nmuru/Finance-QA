## Skill: Synthesis
**Objective:** Produce a balanced, evidence-based diagnostic report.

**Instructions:**
1. Do not include raw JSON.
2. Use Markdown tables for annual comparisons where useful.
3. Clearly label each item as a reported observation, derived metric, or analytical signal.
4. Avoid unsupported causal explanations.
5. Include a concise limitation statement where data coverage is incomplete.
