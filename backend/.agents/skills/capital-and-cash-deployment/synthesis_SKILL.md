## Skill: Synthesis
**Objective:** Produce a concise, readable capital-allocation analysis from retrieved evidence.

**Instructions:**
1. Do not include raw JSON.
2. Present annual quantitative evidence in Markdown tables.
3. Label derived metrics such as free cash flow clearly.
4. Use one decimal place for percentages where percentages are calculated.
5. Separate reported facts, derived calculations, and analytical observations.
6. Preserve important data limitations.
