## Skill: Financing Analysis
**Objective:** Identify how financing activity has affected cash and the balance sheet.

**Instructions:**
1. Review financing cash flows for debt issuance, debt repayment, dividends, and share repurchases where separately reported.
2. Compare gross financing inflows and outflows across years when the statement provides them.
3. Identify years in which financing cash flows materially supported or reduced ending cash.
4. If equity issuance or other financing is not separately disclosed in the retrieved statement, do not assume it occurred.
5. Use targeted XBRL facts only to verify a specific financing item that cannot be established from the statement.
