## Skill: Liquidity Analysis
**Objective:** Assess historical changes in liquidity and funding structure using reported financial statements.

**Instructions:**
1. Retrieve the balance sheet and cash flow statement.
2. Track cash and cash equivalents across the available annual periods.
3. Identify material changes in total debt or major current-liability balances when reported clearly.
4. Where the data permits, calculate simple indicators such as cash change, net debt direction, and current-asset/current-liability coverage.
5. Treat these as historical indicators, not as an unsupported statement about future solvency.
6. Explain large changes using reported statement evidence before considering targeted XBRL facts.
