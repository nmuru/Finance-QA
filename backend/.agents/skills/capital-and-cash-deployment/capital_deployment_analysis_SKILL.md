## Skill: Capital Deployment Analysis
**Objective:** Describe how generated cash has been allocated over time.

**Instructions:**
1. Retrieve the cash flow statement first.
2. Identify material cash uses and sources such as capital expenditures, dividends, share repurchases, acquisitions, debt issuance, and debt repayment when separately disclosed.
3. Compare annual amounts and identify material changes in scale or mix.
4. Distinguish operating investment from shareholder distributions and financing activity.
5. Do not infer an acquisition, repurchase program, or other capital-allocation action merely from a balance-sheet movement when the relevant cash-flow disclosure is absent.
6. Use targeted XBRL retrieval only when a needed item is not readable from the statement.
